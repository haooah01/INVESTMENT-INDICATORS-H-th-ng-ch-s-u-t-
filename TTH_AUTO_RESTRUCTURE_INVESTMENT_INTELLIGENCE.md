## 3. Core Investment Indicator Domains

Normalize indicators under these ten domains:

1.  **Ownership** --- ownership interests, equity, beneficial ownership,
    voting rights, and asset rights.
2.  **Capital** --- capital structure, invested capital, debt, equity,
    commitments, and capital deployment.
3.  **Revenue** --- revenue sources, revenue quality, recurring revenue,
    and growth.
4.  **Profitability** --- gross profit, operating profit, net income,
    and margins.
5.  **Cash Flow** --- operating cash flow, investing cash flow,
    financing cash flow, and free cash flow.
6.  **Assets** --- asset composition, valuation, liquidity, utilization,
    and impairment.
7.  **Efficiency** --- returns on capital, productivity, asset turnover,
    and operating efficiency.
8.  **Growth** --- organic growth, inorganic growth, market expansion,
    and scalability.
9.  **Risk** --- financial, operational, market, liquidity, regulatory,
    country, currency, and systemic risks.
10. **Control & Influence** --- decision rights, governance, operational
    control, capital-allocation authority, and market influence.

## 4. Indicator Reconstruction

For every existing or newly proposed indicator:

-   Identify its canonical name and unique identifier.
-   Define its economic meaning and intended use.
-   Assign it to one primary domain and applicable entity levels.
-   Record its formula, unit, currency, time period, and aggregation
    method.
-   Identify required inputs and data dependencies.
-   Classify it as a leading, lagging, or diagnostic indicator.
-   Mark whether it is directly observed, calculated, estimated, or
    qualitative.
-   Detect duplicates, synonyms, overlapping concepts, and derived
    metrics.
-   Preserve meaningful distinctions instead of merging metrics that
    measure different things.

Do not add an indicator merely to increase the indicator count.

## 5. Entity-Specific Measurement

Adapt the indicator definitions and calculations to the entity being
analyzed.

### L01--L04: Individual and Team

Consider:

-   Net worth and personal balance sheet
-   Income, expenses, savings, and personal cash flow
-   Debt and debt-service obligations
-   Human capital and productive capacity
-   Business-owner earnings
-   Project revenue, cost, margin, and ROI
-   Team productivity and resource utilization

### L05--L08: Enterprise

Consider:

-   Revenue, gross profit, EBITDA, operating profit, and net income
-   Gross, operating, and net margins
-   Working capital and cash conversion
-   CAC, LTV, retention, churn, and customer concentration
-   ROIC, ROCE, asset turnover, and capital productivity
-   Debt ratios, liquidity, solvency, and break-even point
-   Enterprise value and capital-allocation efficiency

### L09--L12: Corporate Groups and Multinationals

Consider:

-   Consolidated revenue, profit, assets, and cash flow
-   Subsidiary-level performance
-   Intercompany transactions and eliminations
-   Minority interests and non-controlling interests
-   Geographic and segment revenue mix
-   Foreign exchange exposure
-   Country and regulatory risks
-   Transfer-pricing exposure
-   Global supply-chain concentration
-   Effective tax rate and cross-border tax exposure
-   Group ROIC and capital allocation

### L13--L16: Ecosystems and Global Capital Networks

Consider:

-   Network effects and ecosystem value creation
-   Partner and platform dependency
-   Assets under management (AUM)
-   Assets under custody (AUC)
-   Net asset value (NAV)
-   Gross asset value (GAV)
-   Committed capital and deployed capital
-   Dry powder
-   Portfolio IRR and MOIC
-   Fund flows and institutional capital allocation
-   Foreign direct investment (FDI)
-   Cross-border capital and trade flows
-   Market concentration and systemic risk

## 6. Ownership, Control, and Influence

Keep these concepts separate:

### Ownership

-   Legal ownership
-   Beneficial ownership
-   Equity percentage
-   Voting rights
-   Economic rights
-   Minority interests
-   Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO), where verifiable

### Control

-   Board appointment rights
-   Voting control
-   Contractual control
-   Operational control
-   Consolidation rights
-   Capital-allocation authority

### Influence

-   Market influence
-   Network position
-   Strategic partnerships
-   Pricing power
-   Supply-chain leverage
-   Standard-setting or platform influence

Never infer ownership or control solely from public visibility,
seniority, wealth estimates, or business relationships.

## 7. Capital and Value-Flow Mapping

Map the economic chain:

`Capital Input → Value Creation → Value Capture → Revenue → Profitability → Cash Flow → Reinvestment → Asset Accumulation`

Also map:

-   Entity → Ownership → Equity → Control
-   Investor → Investment → Portfolio Company → Return
-   Parent Company → Subsidiary → Consolidation
-   Customer → Transaction → Revenue → Retention
-   Capital Provider → Capital Recipient → Deployment → Realized Return
-   Domestic Entity → Foreign Subsidiary → Cross-border Cash Flow

For each relationship, record direction, amount (if known), currency,
date, legal/economic basis, source, and confidence.

Prevent double counting of: - Intercompany revenue and expenses -
Intra-group loans and investments - Parent and subsidiary asset values -
Fund assets and underlying portfolio assets - Gross transaction value
and net economic value

## 8. Formula and Calculation Library

Standardize formulas while documenting their scope and limitations.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Indicator                           Standard formula
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Engagement Rate                     Engagements / defined audience
                                      denominator × 100%

  CTR                                 Clicks / Impressions × 100%

  Conversion Rate                     Conversions / defined eligible
                                      opportunities × 100%

  RPM                                 Revenue / Views × 1,000

  CPM                                 Advertising cost / Impressions ×
                                      1,000

  CAC                                 Acquisition costs / New customers
                                      acquired

  AOV                                 Revenue / Number of orders

  ARPU                                Revenue / Average active users

  Gross Margin                        (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue × 100%

  Net Margin                          Net income / Revenue × 100%

  ROI                                 (Gain from investment − Investment
                                      cost) / Investment cost × 100%

  ROIC                                NOPAT / Average invested capital

  ROE                                 Net income attributable to equity
                                      holders / Average equity

  ROA                                 Net income / Average total assets

  ROCE                                EBIT / Capital employed

  ROAS                                Attributed advertising revenue /
                                      Advertising spend

  LTV                                 Expected lifetime contribution
                                      profit per customer, using an
                                      explicitly stated model

  LTV/CAC                             Customer lifetime value / Customer
                                      acquisition cost

  Payback Period                      CAC / Periodic contribution profit
                                      per acquired customer

  Churn Rate                          Customers lost during period /
                                      Customers at start of period

  FCF                                 Operating cash flow − Capital
                                      expenditures

  EBITDA Margin                       EBITDA / Revenue × 100%

  Debt-to-Equity                      Defined debt measure / Equity

  Asset Turnover                      Revenue / Average total assets
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

For every formula, specify: - Numerator and denominator definitions -
Reporting period - Average versus closing balance convention -
Consolidated versus standalone scope - Currency and conversion date -
Treatment of exceptional items - Data source and calculation status

Do not treat CPM and RPM as interchangeable. Do not treat revenue as
profit, AUM as owned assets, committed capital as deployed capital, or
accounting profit as cash flow.

## 9. Evidence and Data Governance

Assign each data point one status:

-   **VERIFIED** --- supported by a reliable primary source or
    corroborated evidence.
-   **REPORTED** --- stated by the entity or an identified source but
    not independently verified.
-   **ESTIMATED** --- calculated or inferred using a disclosed method
    and assumptions.
-   **UNKNOWN** --- unavailable or not sufficiently supported.
-   **CONFLICTED** --- credible sources disagree.

Every data point should include, where applicable:

-   Source name and source type
-   Source URL or document reference
-   Publication date
-   Observation date
-   Retrieval date
-   Entity identifier
-   Reporting period
-   Currency and unit
-   Methodology
-   Confidence level
-   Reviewer or validation status

Never invent missing values, sources, benchmarks, ownership links, or
financial relationships.

-   Where is value created?
